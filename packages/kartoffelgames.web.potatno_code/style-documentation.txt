-- General
    - Border-radius of 4px.
    - Hover and action transitions times are 0.15s.

-- List.
    - Colored border of 0.25rem on left. Full height (most of the time).
        - After that, an icon with 0.8rem horizontal padding on each side.
    - Padding of 0.8rem to left edge.
        - Is the item selectable, the total padding is devided between, padding 0.5rem & margin 0.3rem
    - Selectable items:
        - Have a border radius.
        - Margin/gap of 0.3rem between them.
        - Padding of 0.3rem 0.6rem 0.3rem 0.5rem;

-- Panels
    - Dividers are 2px height with a 1rem gap on each side.
    - Content has a 1rem top and bottom padding.


-- Actions
Scale to 0.98 or translate 1px, based on looks on clicks.
 - Buttons usually Scale
 - Action such as dropdowns usually translate.

-- Popups
 - Animation time is 0.15s
 - Transition from opacity 0 to 1.
 - Transition from Translate(0 -20|20) to Translate(0 0)
